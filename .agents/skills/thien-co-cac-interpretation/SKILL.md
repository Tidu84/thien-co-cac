## Scope
Use to write or review divination interpretations and AI prompts.

## Guidance
Keep readings warm, respectful, clear, and culturally appropriate in Vietnamese. Separate calculation from symbolic interpretation; never invent input data, chart placements, card draws, or scores. Present divination as reflection or tradition, not guaranteed truth. Avoid definitive health, legal, financial, or safety advice. Explain basis and uncertainty and offer grounded next steps. Review fallback text as well as AI prompts.
